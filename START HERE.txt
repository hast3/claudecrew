CLAUDE CREW - START HERE
========================

You only need to do ONE thing:

   Double-click the file called  INSTALL.bat

Then answer the questions it asks. Press Enter to accept the answer shown in [brackets].
It installs everything and opens Crew in your web browser when it is done.

If Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" box:
   click "More info", then "Run anyway".

You also need a paid Claude account (Pro or Max) to sign in with. Free accounts do not work.
Get one at https://claude.ai

Next time, double-click the "Claude Crew" icon on your Desktop.

If something goes wrong, the installer says what to do. You can run INSTALL.bat again as
many times as you like. It never deletes your settings or chat history.
If you are stuck, send the file install-log.txt (in this folder) to whoever is helping you.

Full guide with pictures-in-words and fixes: docs\SETUP.md
