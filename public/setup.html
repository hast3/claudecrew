Set up Claude Crew

Four quick choices. You can change all of them later.

1. Is Claude ready?

Checking...

Crew needs Claude Code, signed in with a paid Claude account. Open PowerShell, type claude, press Enter and follow the sign-in. Then press Check again.

2. Your name

Crew uses it to say hello and tell the helpers who you are.

3. Helpers for your folders (optional)

You always get an Assistant. To add a helper that works inside a folder (a project, notes, documents), paste the folder path. In File Explorer, click the address bar, press Ctrl+C, then paste here.
